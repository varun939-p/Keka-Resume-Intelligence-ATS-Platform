# Design System: Keka Resume Intelligence

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Recruiter's Instrument"**

Resume Intelligence is an operating surface, not a showcase. It reads like a well-made instrument panel in the Keka HR and Linear tradition of executive restraint, cast in petrol and brass: a night-petrol rail, a warm bone ground, white work cards, 1px warm hairlines, graphite ink, and Mona Sans set small and even. Density is high and calm. Table rows are 56px, table text is 13px, and hierarchy comes from weight and ink step rather than from size.

Colour is information. One petrol accent marks what is selected, what acts, what has focus and what is progressing; a brass mark belongs to the rail alone. Five stage pigments (viridian, cobalt, yellow ochre, mulberry for duplicates and a recessive stone) are built in OKLCH at muted chroma, so there is no traffic-light green and red; they are validated for deutan, protan, tritan and normal vision in both themes, and appear only where a status is reported, never without a text label and a drawn glyph. Errors take their own burnt sienna, never shared with duplicates. Numbers are set in the same Mona Sans as the words, always in proportional lining figures, because its tabular set is code-styled (slashed zero, slab-serif one); numeric columns stay right-aligned so units line up. Fragment Mono is reserved for strings a machine wrote: hashes, file paths, event keys, the run log, key caps. The system ships light and dark themes from one paired token set, and the rail stays night petrol in both.

The signature is the routing ruler: a 0 to 100 track split into the Rejected, Hold, Technical Review and Shortlisted bands at the job description's own thresholds, with a graphite mark at the score. It appears compact in every table row and comparison column, full in the candidate drawer, and as the axis of the fit-distribution chart where each candidate is a dot. The owner has ruled out neon purple or cyan gradients, glowing borders, emoji as icons, uneven card grids, Tailwind-default palettes, and fonts or number styles that read as machine-generated, which is why Inter and monospaced numerals left the system.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Night-petrol rail, bone ground, white cards, 1px warm hairlines; flat at rest.
- One petrol accent on the page; brass only in the rail.
- Colour-blind-safe stage pigments reserved for status, always with text and a drawn glyph; rejected is a recessive stone, errors a separate burnt sienna.
- Mona Sans for every word and every number; Fragment Mono only for machine strings.
- Proportional lining figures everywhere; right-aligned columns keep units in line.
- Dense, table-first layout on even card grids; the routing ruler carries every score.
- Light and dark themes from paired tokens; the rail stays night petrol in both.

## Colors

Warm bone neutrals and graphite ink around a night-petrol rail, one petrol accent, a brass brand mark held in the rail, five colour-blind-safe stage pigments held in reserve for routing, and a burnt-sienna error colour. Every token has a dark-theme sibling (suffix `-dark` in the frontmatter) applied under `[data-theme="dark"]`; brass, rail-ink, rail-ok and the translucent rail tokens are shared.

### Primary
- **Petrol** (`accent`, #145a6b; dark #63b3c4): selection and action. Fills the primary button, progress bars, completed pipeline steps, score-dimension bars and comparison bars. Draws the 2px focus outline, the 3px input focus ring (18%), the active drawer-tab underline, checkbox, radio and range accents, the text caret, the manual-override dot, the latest timeline node and the menu check. As a 6 to 9% tint it marks selected and open table rows, checked options, the bulk-action bar, the workspace banner and a dropzone with a file over it; text selection is a 24% tint.
- **Deep Petrol** (`accent-ink`, #0f4c5b; dark #8fcad7): the accent as text. Links, link buttons, nice-to-have skill chips, event keys in the run log, the workspace tag.
- **On Petrol** (`on-accent`, #ffffff; dark #0b1a1d): text and icons on a filled accent. It flips to near-black in dark because the lighter dark-theme petrol needs dark text; both hold above 7:1.

### Secondary
- **Brass Mark** (`brass`, #c9a24a, both themes): the brand, not an accent. Fills the 28px brand mark (drawn with a night-petrol stroke) and the favicon, colours the current page's icon in the rail, and draws the rail's focus outline. It holds 6.3:1 against the rail and does not appear on the page plane.

### Status (routing bands)
The stage colours are a pigment set built in OKLCH at muted chroma. Each stage has a mark colour (`band-*`: ruler segments, distribution dots and bands, legend dots) and four tag tokens on equal OKLCH lightness steps, so the five read as one graded family: `st-*` for text, `tint-*` for the tag fill, `edge-*` for the 1px inset edge and `gl-*` for the glyph. Text holds at least 6.6:1 on its own tint in light and 9.1:1 in dark. The chromatic four light marks pass the dataviz palette checker on all pairs (CVD ΔE 12.0 deutan, 16.8 for normal vision); the dark marks are their own validated set (CVD ΔE 10.9, normal 15.4, all at least 3:1), not a flip.
- **Viridian** (`band-short` #288e70, dark #3fa484; `st-short` #0f5642, dark #9ae1c6): Shortlisted. Also matched skills, positive deltas, completed runs, ok badges, and the "Best" value in a comparison.
- **Cobalt** (`band-review` #3660a3, dark #5883c0; `st-review` #23457b, dark #b1d0fc): Technical Review, and a batch that is still running. It is a different hue from petrol, so the accent and this stage never share a colour.
- **Yellow Ochre** (`band-hold` #d1a55a, dark #b68525; `st-hold` #6d4f19, dark #f4ce90): Hold / Borderline. Also missing core skills, the critical-gap line on a row, possible duplicates, skipped files, warning callouts and the top-gaps ranking bars. As a light-theme mark it is 2.27:1 against white, accepted because a label always accompanies it; its glyph and the attention signal use the deeper `gl-hold` #ab7d25.
- **Stone** (`band-rejected` #a6a199, dark #6c6863; `st-rejected` #56524b, dark #cbc6c0): Rejected. Deliberately recessive: rejection is an outcome, not an alarm, so as a mark it is always backed by its position on the ruler, the tag's glyph and its text. Its tag takes the same tint, inset edge and glyph treatment as the other stages.
- **Mulberry** (`band-dup` #823a5c, dark #9b5375; `st-dup` #6f2d4c, dark #f5bdd4): Duplicate Flagged, and only duplicates: the Duplicate Flagged tag, dedup trace matches, the dedup "Duplicate" and "Match" badges, a matching dedup layer, the duplicate notice in the drawer, the ruler mark of a duplicate, the hollow duplicate dots in the distribution chart, and duplicate events in the run log and audit trail.
- **Burnt Sienna** (`danger` #ac4c2d, dark #e07655; `st-danger` #87381f, dark #ffb7a0; with `tint-danger` and `edge-danger`): errors, never shared with duplicates. Failed files and failed pipeline steps, the knockout "capped" tag, missing critical skills, page errors, danger buttons and destructive actions, failed, deleted and reset events in the run log and audit trail, and the toast error icon.
- **Signals** (`ok` #288e70, `warn` #ab7d25, `danger` #ac4c2d; dark #3fa484, #b68525, #e07655): viridian, deep ochre and burnt sienna reused as plain pass, attention and fail for deltas, progress completion, run results, danger buttons and page errors. The rail has its own pass colour, `rail-ok` (#6ac6a6, both themes), for the live-scan dot and connected status dots.

### Neutral
- **Bone Ground** (`page` #f3f2ee; dark #101413): the app background behind cards. The sticky topbar is this colour at 92% over an 8px blur.
- **Card White** (`card` #ffffff; dark #171c1b): every work surface (cards, table, drawer, menus, dialogs), and the 2px halo that lets ruler marks and distribution dots cut through the bands.
- **Soft Bone** (`soft` #eeede8; dark #202625): hover fill, segmented-control tracks, neutral chips and badges, avatars, skeleton blocks, progress and bar tracks.
- **Sunken Bone** (`sunken` #f8f7f4; dark #141918): the table header row, the comparison label column, disabled inputs, read-only notes, callouts, key caps.
- **Graphite Ink** (`ink` #1c1b19; dark #eaeeec): primary text and the ruler's score mark. Never pure black.
- **Graphite Two** (`ink-2` #45423c; dark #c2c9c6): secondary text: field labels, numeric table cells, comparison values, recommendation prose, avatar initials.
- **Warm Muted** (`muted` #68645c; dark #8e9895): meta lines, sub-lines, column headers, icons at rest, axis labels. Holds at least 5.0:1 on every neutral surface in both themes.
- **Hairline** (`line`, graphite at 10%; dark white at 8%): structural edges. Card borders, row and section dividers, the splits inside stat strips and the comparison grid.
- **Strong Hairline** (`line-strong`, graphite at 18%; dark white at 16%): edges you can operate (buttons, inputs, select buttons), dashed thresholds and the dropzone, the timeline spine, the scrollbar thumb.
- **Night Petrol Rail** (`rail` #0f2a31; dark #0a1a1e) with `rail-ink`, `rail-muted`, `rail-line`, `rail-hover` and `rail-active`: the navigation rail is dark in both themes.
- **Petrol Graphite** (`tip` #16313a; dark #24363b) with `on-tip` #f4f4f6: tooltips and toasts, the only dark floating surfaces in the light theme. The toast error icon uses the dark-theme Burnt Sienna text value (#ffb7a0); the success icon uses the dark-theme Viridian text value (#9ae1c6).

### Named Rules
**The One Petrol Rule.** Petrol is the only accent on the page plane. It marks what is selected, what acts, what has focus and what is progressing; it never fills a decorative block, and data series that are not status (the four dimension weights) are tints of it at 100, 65, 40 and 20%, never a new hue.

**The Brass-In-The-Rail Rule.** Brass is the brand mark: the mark itself, the current page's rail icon and the rail's focus ring. It never crosses onto the page plane, where Yellow Ochre already means borderline.

**The Status-Only Hue Rule.** Viridian, cobalt, yellow ochre, stone, mulberry and burnt sienna appear only where a status is being reported: a stage, a dedup verdict, a skill match, gap or miss, a delta, a best value, a run or file result, an error. Mulberry speaks only for duplicates and burnt sienna only for errors. Never as decoration, never as identity; avatars stay neutral.

**The Never-Alone Rule.** A stage hue always travels with its text label and a drawn glyph. Tags, stage filter tabs and "Move to stage" menus carry the words beside the glyph: a solid disc with a knocked-out check for Shortlisted and with a knocked-out cross for Rejected (the decisions), a ring with a 3/4 Harvey ball for Technical Review and with a 1/2 Harvey ball for Hold (the open stages), and a copy mark for Duplicate, because a duplicate is a flag, not a grade. Chart legends pair the words with dots, a hollow ring marks duplicate dots in charts, and skill chips carry a check or cross icon.

## Typography

**Display Font:** none separate; the largest type on any screen is a score numeral in Mona Sans.
**Body Font:** Mona Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), variable weight 400 to 700 and width 75 to 125 (the Google Fonts request loads the `wdth` axis), lining proportional figures throughout.
**Label/Mono Font:** Fragment Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace), for machine strings only.

**Character:** One sturdy grotesque does all the talking and all the counting, so a score reads as part of the sentence instead of as code. A quiet monospace appears only where a machine wrote the string, which makes the line between evidence a person reads and identifiers a system emits visible at a glance.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Mona Sans 650, 44px, line-height 1, -0.03em, proportional figures): the overall fit score in the candidate drawer, with a 15px muted "/100".
- **Metric** (Mona Sans 600, 28px, line-height 1.1, -0.02em, proportional figures; 22px under 560px): KPI ribbon values, with a 14px muted unit. The comparison's overall-fit figure is a 26px 650 cut of the same style.
- **Headline** (Mona Sans 650, 18px, -0.01em; 17.5px on phones): the page title in the topbar. The candidate name in the drawer is 18px 600 at -0.015em. The largest UI text on any screen.
- **Title** (Mona Sans 600, 14px, -0.005em): card, panel and run headings; dialog titles step up to 16px, the comparison title to 16px 650.
- **Body** (Mona Sans 400, 14px, line-height 1.45): base text. Recommendation prose runs 13.5px at 1.6 with a 70ch cap; dialog prose 13.5px at 1.55; empty-state copy caps at 46ch.
- **Item** (Mona Sans 500, 13.5px, line-height 1.35): rail navigation, option and provider titles.
- **Control** (Mona Sans 500, 13px): buttons, select buttons, segmented controls.
- **Table** (Mona Sans 400, 13px): table cells, dense lists, menu items, toasts; the candidate name steps up to 500.
- **Label** (Mona Sans 500, 12.5px): KPI labels, field labels, toolbar, card and comparison meta, the comparison row labels.
- **Tag** (Mona Sans 500, 12px): chips, column headers. Drawer section headings use 12px 600 muted with +0.02em tracking.
- **Badge** (Mona Sans 580, 12px, line-height 1, `font-stretch: 94%`, +0.004em): stage tags and status badges, semi-condensed and set like a tag.
- **Caption** (Mona Sans 400, 12px): sub-lines under names and KPIs, hints, tooltips.
- **Figure** (Mona Sans 600, 14px, proportional lining figures): the fit numeral in each row, right-aligned. The same cut, at 11 to 18px and 400 to 600, sets column scores, counts, latencies, run stats, axis and threshold ticks, timestamps in lists, deltas, weight percentages and comparison values. Columns stay right-aligned so units line up; stage-tab counts hold a 1.3ch and run-stat values a 3ch minimum width so live counters don't jitter.
- **Machine** (Fragment Mono 400, 11.5px): hashes, file paths, event keys, the run log (at 1.75 line-height), environment variable names, key caps (11px).

### Named Rules
**The Figures Rule.** Numbers are set in Mona Sans, never in a monospace, and always in proportional lining figures. Mona Sans's tabular set is code-styled (a slashed zero and a slab-serif one), so it is never switched on, not even in columns or live counters. A number that stands alone (the drawer score, a KPI value, the comparison's overall fit, a number in running text) reads like type; numbers that line up with others in a column (table scores, counts, latencies, axis ticks, timestamps, deltas, comparison values) stay right-aligned so their units line up; counters that tick while you watch hold a minimum width instead (1.3ch for stage-tab counts, 3ch for run stats).

**The Machine-String Rule.** Fragment Mono sets only strings a machine wrote: hashes, file paths, event keys, the run log, environment variable names and key caps. It never sets a quantity a person reads, a label, a heading or a sentence.

**The Small Ramp Rule.** UI text lives between 11px and 18px. Hierarchy comes from weight (400, 500, 600, 650) and ink step (Graphite Ink, Graphite Two, Warm Muted); only score numerals go larger. Nothing is uppercased, and tracking tightens only on headings and score numerals.

## Layout

The shell is a fixed night-petrol rail (232px) on the left and a main column with a sticky 60px topbar. Content sits in a single-column grid with 16px gaps, padded 24px on top and 28px at the sides, capped at 1640px. Every grid child is `minmax(0, 1fr)` so long lines truncate or scroll inside their cell instead of widening the page.

The Candidates view stacks: a view head (scope selector and its meta on the left, Sync and Export on the right), the KPI ribbon, the candidate table card (toolbar, optional bulk bar, table, footer), then a pair of chart panels at 1.35 : 1. List-and-detail views use a fixed column beside a fluid one: Batch scan puts a 320 to 400px configuration column beside the run card (sticky 84px from the top above 1121px); Job descriptions puts a 260 to 320px list beside the editor. Integrations is two equal cards whose footers pin to the bottom so the row stays even. The comparison dialog lays candidates out as a 168px label column beside two to four equal columns (at least 190px each) that scroll inside the dialog.

Density is set by row height: 56px candidate rows, 44px compact rows (audit, environment, run files), 36px table headers. Above 1280px the candidate table header sticks under the topbar.

Responsive steps:
- **1360px:** the Impact and Education score columns drop; the scan configuration column narrows to 300 to 360px.
- **1280px:** the Critical skills column drops.
- **1120px:** chart panels, settings cards and both list-and-detail views stack to one column; the KPI ribbon and run stats reflow to three across; table rows show the fit numeral without the compact ruler.
- **860px:** the rail becomes a 260px off-canvas sheet behind a menu button and a scrim; gutters drop to 16px; search and stage tabs each take a full toolbar row; the topbar scan button collapses to its icon; the Dedup column drops; the drawer's two-column grids stack.
- **560px:** KPI and run stats go two across with the last cell spanning; numeric score columns and avatars drop; stage tabs become a three-column grid; badges switch to short labels; the full ruler hides its band names.

**The Even Grid Rule.** Peer cards share one track rule and one height: equal fractions (KPI cells, run stats, settings cards, comparison columns) or the single 1.35 : 1 chart pair. List-and-detail views put a fixed column beside a fluid one, top-aligned. No staggered, masonry or ad-hoc mosaic card grids.

## Elevation & Depth

The page plane is flat. Depth comes from tone (bone ground, white card, sunken header, soft hover) and from 1px hairlines; cards, tables and the KPI ribbon cast no shadow. Shadows appear only on surfaces that float above the page and will be dismissed: menus, the confirm and comparison dialogs, the candidate drawer, tooltips, toasts and the off-canvas rail. In the light theme the large shadows are tinted night petrol rather than black. Scrims dim the page under the drawer (`rgb(10 11 14 / 0.28)`), the off-canvas rail (0.35) and the dialogs (0.4). The topbar is the only translucent surface.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgb(15 42 49 / 0.24), 0 0 0 1px rgb(28 27 25 / 0.08)`; dark `0 16px 40px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6), 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 0.08)`): menus and both dialogs. The 1px ring stands in for a border.
- **Drawer** (`box-shadow: -24px 0 48px -24px rgb(15 42 49 / 0.32)`; dark `-24px 0 48px -16px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`): the candidate drawer, cast leftward onto the page.
- **Rail sheet** (`box-shadow: 24px 0 48px -24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the off-canvas rail under 860px.
- **Tooltip** (`box-shadow: 0 6px 16px -6px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35)`) and **Toast** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -10px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): small dark floating surfaces.
- **Segment lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.08), 0 0 0 1px var(--line)`): the selected segment of the mode switch and stage tabs.
- **Card halo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--card)`): a card-coloured ring around ruler marks and distribution dots so they separate from the bands beneath.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Nothing on the page plane casts a shadow; cards separate from the ground by tone and a hairline. A shadow means the surface floats and will go away. The selected-segment lift is the one in-plane exception, and it marks a choice, not a container.

## Shapes

Corners shrink with the element: dialogs 14px, cards 12px, inset panels inside cards (recommendation, dedup layers, contact grid, metrics strip, dropzone, menus, toasts) 10px, controls and row avatars 8px, rail items and the brand mark 7px, chips, segments and menu items 6px, stage tags and status badges 5px, small tags 4px, ruler bands 2px. The drawer's large avatar is 44px with 11px corners. Nested corners are concentric: a 6px segment sits in a 9px track with 3px padding. Status dots, badge dots, distribution dots and timeline nodes are full circles.

Edges are 1px hairlines everywhere: `line` where an edge divides, `line-strong` where an edge can be operated. Dashed strokes carry a specific meaning: a drop target (the dropzone's 1.5px dashed outline, the chip input), a threshold (distribution chart), or a skill that is listed but not demonstrated (dashed match chip). Groups of related figures are split by internal hairlines inside one container (run stats, pipeline steps, the drawer metrics strip, the contact grid, the comparison grid), and bands that form one scale are split by 2px seams (ruler segments, distribution bands, the weight stack).

Stage glyphs form a small vocabulary, drawn as 12px SVG in the stage's `gl-*` colour: a solid disc with a knocked-out check (Shortlisted) or a solid stone disc with a knocked-out cross (Rejected) for the two decisions; a ring holding a 3/4 Harvey ball (Technical Review) or a 1/2 Harvey ball (Hold / Borderline) for the two open stages; and a copy mark of two overlapping rounded squares for Duplicate Flagged, because a duplicate is a flag, not a grade. The same glyphs mark the stage filter tabs and the "Move to stage" menus. Non-stage status badges keep a 6px round dot unless `plain`, and duplicate dots in the distribution chart are a hollow 2px ring.

**The Hairline Rule.** Structure is drawn with 1px lines. Heavier strokes are reserved for state (the 2px focus outline, the 2px active-tab underline, the 2px duplicate ring) and for the dropzone's 1.5px dashed target.

**The Shrinking Corner Rule.** A smaller element never has a larger radius than its container; nested radii step down by the padding between them.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every word and every number in Mona Sans in proportional lining figures, right-align numeric columns so units line up, and give live counters a minimum width so they don't jitter.
- **Do** reserve Fragment Mono for hashes, file paths, event keys, the run log, environment variable names and key caps.
- **Do** draw structure with 1px warm hairlines (`line` to divide, `line-strong` for edges that can be operated) on bone and white surfaces, and keep cards flat at rest.
- **Do** keep Petrol for selection, action, focus and progress, and fill only the button that moves the story forward in its region.
- **Do** keep Brass to the brand mark, the current page's rail icon and the rail focus ring.
- **Do** show a stage with its pigment, its drawn glyph and its text label together, using that stage's `st-*`, `tint-*`, `edge-*` and `gl-*` tokens.
- **Do** carry every fit score on the routing ruler at the job description's own thresholds: compact in rows and comparison columns, full in the drawer, as dots in the distribution.
- **Do** flag duplicates and critical gaps on the candidate's own row (dedup trace, yellow-ochre gap line, capped tag), not in a separate report.
- **Do** ship every surface in both themes from the paired tokens, and keep the rail night petrol in both.
- **Do** keep card grids even, with grid children at `minmax(0, 1fr)` so long lines truncate instead of widening the page.
- **Do** keep transitions between 0.12s and 0.26s on the shared ease-out curve and honour reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gradients, neon purple or cyan, glows or glowing borders. The only halo in the system is the focus ring on inputs (3px) and range thumbs (4px).
- **Don't** use emoji or text glyphs as icons; icons are 1.75px-stroke SVG line icons at 14, 16 or 20px.
- **Don't** use viridian, cobalt, yellow ochre, stone, mulberry or burnt sienna for decoration, identity or emphasis, don't use mulberry for errors or burnt sienna for duplicates, and don't tint avatars.
- **Don't** introduce a second page accent; non-status series are tints of Petrol, and Brass stays in the rail.
- **Don't** use Tailwind's default emerald, blue, amber and red for stages; a new status colour must pass the same deutan, protan and tritan check as the existing four.
- **Don't** set numbers, labels, headings or prose in Fragment Mono, and don't uppercase labels.
- **Don't** use pure black for ink or cool zinc greys for the ground and hairlines; the neutrals are warm bone and graphite.
- **Don't** put shadows on cards, tables or the KPI ribbon; shadows belong to what floats.
- **Don't** build uneven, staggered or masonry card grids.
- **Don't** treat the KPI ribbon's single hairline-split card as a template for other card groups.
